**SUPPORT CARD BUILDS**
You will need:
1. Goo goo babies: Have or borrow Super Creek SSR, you want Swinging Maestro it's the best recovery skill in the game.
2. OPTIONALLY one Wit card, I like having it. If you have Fine Motion SSR you can use hers I heard it's good
3. IF YOUR HORSE RUNS LONG RACES add another Stamina card
4. Fill the rest with speed cards, I like to use Sweep Tosho and event Special Week (especially if your build is Pace Chaser) Don't worry about Power because you'll get enough from Speed friendship training.

**SKILLS (I am paraphrasing from a Reddit skill build guide)**
- Everyone wants Swinging Maestro (Corner recovery)
- If you inherit Oguri Cap's skill, buy it
- If you inherit El Condor Pasa or Taiki Shuttle's skill and your build is Front Runner or Pace Chaser, buy it
- Buy green skills Firm Conditions and Lone Wolf if you get them
- Buy Slipstream and Ramp Up if you get them
- Buy Hydrate/Gourmand if your build is Pace Chaser
- Refer to the skill build guide for details I don't remember most of it and these are the skills I personally see due to the support cards I use.

**TRAINING/RACES**
- Early game just build friendship.
- Once you start getting friendship training/orange gauges, focus on that, do Speed > Stamina > Wit if there are multiple activations.
- When there's no friendship training train Wit (for more SP, Speed, 450 wit for 80% skill activation) or you can rest I guess.
